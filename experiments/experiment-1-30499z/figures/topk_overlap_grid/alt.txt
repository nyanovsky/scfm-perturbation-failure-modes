Median overlap of the two split halves' top-k gene sets, over k = 10, 50, 100 and 500 (log axis), by effect-size stratum and cell line. The dashed line is the chance level k/n for n expressed genes, the open square is the k the report quotes for that stratum, and the cross is the median adaptive k, which is each perturbation's own differential-expression count clipped to [10, 500]. The lower panels rescale the same numbers as excess over chance, where 0 is chance and 1 is a perfectly reproduced set. Raw overlap climbs with k largely because chance climbs with k; the excess does not.
