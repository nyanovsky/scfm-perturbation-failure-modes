Four panels in two rows, HepG2 on the left and Jurkat on the right. The top row is the off-target hidden-state change divided by the change at the perturbed gene's own position, a dimensionless ratio, drawn for scGPT and scPRINT; TranscriptFormer has no own-position value, so its ratio is undefined and the panel says so in its colour instead of drawing a line. The bottom row is the absolute off-target norm, which all three models have, each in its own hidden units, so profiles are compared by shape and not by height. Both rows plot against transformer block on a logarithmic vertical axis: a solid line with a group-bootstrap band for the zero-out query and four thin lines for the other doses. Layer 0 is exactly zero by construction and is stated in each panel rather than plotted, and hollow markers are values clipped to the axis floor.
