Four panels in two rows: split-half retrieval on top, the trained held-out-cell probe below, HepG2 on the left and Jurkat on the right. Each panel plots top-1 accuracy at recovering which gene was perturbed against transformer block, on a logarithmic vertical axis, one line per model with its group-bootstrap band. A dotted line marks chance, one over the number of perturbations, and a grey strip along the bottom spans the largest shuffled-label draw in that panel. Hollow markers are scores of zero clipped to the axis floor; a model that was not run in a panel, or whose score is undefined, is named there with the reason.
